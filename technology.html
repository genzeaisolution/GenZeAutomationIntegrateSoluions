STACK
ENGINEERING

Built on the languages your infrastructure team already trusts.

We pick the stack to match your existing environment and hiring pool, not the other way around. Every layer below is in active use across our deployments.

Backend

Node.js, .NET, PHP and Python — chosen per project based on your team's existing skills and the performance profile of the module being built.

Frontend

Next.js and React for agent-facing dashboards and customer portals that need to stay fast under real call-floor load.

Databases

Microsoft SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle and MariaDB. We match the engine to the deployment — enterprise, open-source or already-standardized — rather than forcing one default.

Communications

WebRTC and SIP for softphone and telephony connectivity, built to sit in front of whichever PBX or carrier you're already using.

AI & data

Python-based model pipelines, vector search for knowledge retrieval, and message-queue architecture for real-time agentic workflows.
