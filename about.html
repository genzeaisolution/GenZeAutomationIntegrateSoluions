ABOUT GAIS

An automation and software team built around one problem: disconnected systems.

GenZe Automation Integrate Solutions started from a simple observation — most contact centers run their telephony, CRM and workforce tools as separate products that were never meant to talk to each other. We build them as one. Founded and led by CEO Moiz Khan, GAIS is the team behind Vertox and the rest of the product family on this site.

WHO WE ARE

Engineers first

We are a development team, not a reseller. Every module in the Vertox suite, and every custom build we deliver, is written and maintained by GAIS engineers on Node.js, .NET, PHP, Python and Next.js.

WHAT WE VALUE

Systems that fit, not systems that impress

We design around how your team already works — your database, your call flows, your compliance requirements — rather than asking you to adapt to ours.
